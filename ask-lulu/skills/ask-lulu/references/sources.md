# Sources

The bibliography is grouped by what each source grounds. Each entry records one short
quotation approved for in-character citation. **Primary** means Lulu's own publication
or an interview transcript of her speech. **Mirror** means the transcript is hosted by
a third party and may contain transcription errors. Dates are publication dates when
the page supplies one; year-only dates reflect the available episode metadata.

## Founder ownership, strategy, and tactics

1. **Primary — Lulu Cheng Meservey, “Go Direct: The Manifesto” — 2024-03-19.**
   Defines founder ownership, direct audience relationships, preserved sharp edges, and
   the boundary between going direct and going alone.
   [Source](https://www.getflack.com/p/go-direct-the-manifesto)
   > “Going direct means crafting and telling your own story, without being dependent on intermediaries.”

2. **Primary — Lulu Cheng Meservey, “Comms Foundation #1: Strategy” — 2022-08-23.**
   Grounds purpose, business ends, focus, execution, and the rejection of isolated comms goals.
   [Source](https://www.getflack.com/p/comms-foundation-1-strategy)
   > “Purpose is the difference between strategic comms and random noise.”

3. **Primary — Lulu Cheng Meservey, “Comms Foundation #2: Tactics” — 2022-09-08.**
   Supplies the belief-audience-action-objective formula, master narrative, proof, and channel fit.
   [Source](https://www.getflack.com/p/tactics)
   > “Now you write your master narrative if you don’t already have one.”

4. **Primary — Lulu Cheng Meservey, “Should you hire a PR agency?” — 2025-03-14.**
   Distinguishes founder-owned story and strategy from execution that can sensibly be delegated.
   [Source](https://www.getflack.com/p/should-you-hire-a-pr-agency)
   > “Even if it’s mid, at least it’ll be mid in your voice.”

## Coverage gaps

- Most evidence concerns technology companies, founders, media, launches, crises, and
  audience-building. Advice for government, nonprofits, internal people management,
  consumer support, or intimate personal communication is extrapolation.
- The manifesto rubric in `taste-profile.md` is a synthesis across sources, not a
  published Lulu checklist. In particular, the explicit CTA distinction is inferred
  from her Rostra manifesto (which notes it has no CTA) and operational comms guidance.
- The Lenny, How I Write, Uncapped, and FIRE pages are third-party transcripts or mirrors.
  Treat punctuation and marginal wording as less reliable than Lulu's own writing.
- Examples in Lulu's public work often come from organizations she advised or led, but
  this skill does not claim private client facts, confidential methods, or endorsements.
- Novel product, design, policy, or cultural judgments should be reasoned from the
  documented worldview and never attributed as positions Lulu has publicly taken.
